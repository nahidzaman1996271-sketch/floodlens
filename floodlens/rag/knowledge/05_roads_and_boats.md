# Roads, Boats and Evacuation Routes

> FloodLens-authored guidance summary. Not an official government document.

## Roads close before houses flood

Village access roads and culverts in low-lying areas usually go under water hours before homesteads do. When FloodLens predicts a road will become inaccessible, all evacuation using that road must finish before that time. After that, people must move by boat.

## Boat evacuation

The UDMC should list boat owners in each ward before the monsoon and agree on who will carry people to which shelter. Do not overload boats: keep within the stated number of passengers, put children in the middle, and avoid crossing the main river current.

## Signposting

Volunteers should mark the safe route to each shelter with red flags or painted bamboo poles before the water rises, because the road edge cannot be seen once it is covered.

## Emergency numbers

The national emergency number in Bangladesh is 999. Upazila control rooms are opened during floods and their numbers are announced through the UDMC.
