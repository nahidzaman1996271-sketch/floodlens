# Livestock, Crops and Household Assets

> FloodLens-authored guidance summary. Not an official government document.

## Livestock

Cattle, goats and poultry are a household's main savings in rural Bangladesh. If the alert gives more than six hours, move animals to a raised killa (earthen mound), embankment or the high ground next to the shelter. Take enough dry fodder and straw for several days, because grazing land will be under water. Never tie animals inside the house, where they can drown.

## Grain and seed

Store rice, seed and fertiliser in sealed drums or bags on a raised platform (macha) at least one metre above the expected flood level. Seed for the next season is more important than stored food, because food aid is usually available but seed is not.

## Household items

Lift the tube-well head, solar panel batteries and important tools onto the macha or roof. Switch off the electricity at the meter before leaving.

## When time is short

People come before animals and assets. If the alert says flooding is expected within three hours, leave immediately and do not go back for livestock.
