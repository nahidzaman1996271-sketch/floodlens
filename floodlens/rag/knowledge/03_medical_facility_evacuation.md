# Medical Facility Flood Response

> FloodLens-authored guidance summary. Not an official government document.

## Priority

Community clinics, union health and family welfare centres and upazila health complexes that are forecast to flood get the highest evacuation priority, because their patients cannot move quickly and because the facility is needed after the flood.

## Before water arrives

Move patients who cannot walk, newborns and women in labour first, using boats arranged in advance with the UDMC. Move vaccines and insulin in cold boxes with ice packs, along with essential medicines, delivery kits and patient registers, to the upper floor or to the designated relocation site.

## Continuity of care

Health workers should set up a temporary treatment point inside the nearest large shelter, stocked with ORS, zinc, antibiotics for prescribed patients, wound dressing and snake-bite referral information, because snake bites increase sharply during floods.

## Communication

The facility in-charge should tell the upazila health office where the temporary treatment point is located and how it can be reached.
