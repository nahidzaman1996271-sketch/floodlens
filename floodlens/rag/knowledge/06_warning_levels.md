# Understanding River Flood Warning Levels

> FloodLens-authored guidance summary. Not an official government document.

## Where forecasts come from

In Bangladesh, river level forecasts are produced by the Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre (FFWC) of the Bangladesh Water Development Board. FloodLens uses gauge readings and rainfall forecasts to estimate when a river station will cross its danger level and how the water will spread through the local landscape.

## Levels used by FloodLens

The danger level is the water level at a gauge above which flooding of the surrounding area is expected. FloodLens also uses a severe level, above which widespread and deep flooding is likely. For each building, the time until water reaches it decides its risk level: CRITICAL for 10 hours or less, HIGH for up to 24 hours, WATCH for more than 24 hours inside the forecast window, and SAFE when no flooding is forecast within 72 hours.

## Uncertainty

Every forecast carries an uncertainty band, shown as plus or minus metres. When the upper end of the band crosses the danger level, households should already prepare, even if the central forecast stays below it.

## Rainfall

Heavy rainfall upstream, including in India's Meghalaya and Assam, can raise rivers in north-eastern Bangladesh within a day. A forecast can therefore worsen quickly, and alerts are re-issued every hour.
